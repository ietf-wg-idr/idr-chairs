# Flowspec Work List
Work largely pending sufficient completion of FSv2 core drafts so these can proceed.
Steps for Flow specification are: 
1. WG LC and Publish FSv1 Extended Community drafts with actions (in process)  
2. Revise the [draft-ietf-idr-fsv2-ip-basic]- for Filters (done), Extended Community actions (done), Open capabilities (in process) 
3. Do initial adoption call for FSv2 filters (done) and Extended Community Actions (in process) 
4. Subsequent calls for FSv2 filters and FS Extended community actions 
5. Revise FSv2 for ordered actions [draft-hares-idr-fsv2-ip-more-actions] - 
6. Do second adoption call for FSv2 actions that require actions
